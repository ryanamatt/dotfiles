Arcane Theme Reference

CONCEPT

  A fantasy / otherworldly aesthetic built around two anchor colors from
  the existing system setup, extended into a full cohesive palette.

  The feeling: a void between dimensions — deep purple-black darkness,
  arcane magic glowing in magenta and lavender, gold runes, mint-green
  success signals, and rose-red warnings. Stars scattered through empty
  space. Crystal towers. A world that exists just past the edge of sleep.

EXTENDING THE THEME TO NEW APPS

  When theming a new application, follow this decision tree:

  Background?       → void #0d0010 or deep #1a0028
  Surface/card?     → deep #1a0028 or shadow #2a0e3a
  Text?             → petal #e8c5e9 (primary)  mist #8a6f99 (secondary)
  Accent/highlight? → magenta #b00ea2 (strong)  mallow #ac74ad (soft)
  Bright accent?    → fae #d966f5
  Border?           → magenta #b00ea2 (active)  shadow #2a0e3a (inactive)
  Hover?            → dusk #3b1f4a
  Success?          → green #5ecfa0
  Warning?          → gold #f5c842
  Error?            → red #e05c7a
  Info?             → cyan #9de8e8
  Shadow/glow?      → rgba(b00ea2, 0.25–0.40)

  Readability note (added after the Obsidian theme build):
  raw mist/dusk/shadow/magenta/void read beautifully on a terminal
  bar or status line where the surrounding chrome is small and the
  eye isn't lingering, but they get muddy fast — or in void's case,
  tiring to read against — as body text, long-form borders, or any
  surface read continuously for more than a second (reading apps,
  editors, docs). For those contexts, swap in the derived tones
  instead:
    main reading surface   → paper #15131c      (not raw void)
    secondary/muted text   → mist-text #a08bb0  (not raw mist)
    inactive borders/hr    → border-soft #443a52 (not raw shadow)
    visible/hover borders  → border-strong #5a4d6b (not raw dusk)
    accent needing to read
      at text size (links,
      headings, focus rings) → fae-accent #c93dd6 or
                                 link-heading #e58af8 (not raw magenta)
  Rule of thumb: if it's a UI accent glanced at (status icon, bar
  segment) use the raw palette; if it's read continuously (body
  text, link color, a border you have to find, the page you write
  on) use the lifted/desaturated tone.

  Rule: pure black (#000000) and pure white (#ffffff) are never used.
  The darkest point is void #0d0010, lightest is petal #e8c5e9.
  Every color has a purple undertone — even the greens and cyans lean
  slightly cool/purple rather than warm/yellow.
