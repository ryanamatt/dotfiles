Grotto Theme Reference

CONCEPT

  A hidden-waterfall / jungle-grotto aesthetic built around the colors of
  a sunlit cascade tucked deep in a mossy ravine — extended into a full
  cohesive palette.

  The feeling: standing at the edge of a pool where a waterfall drops
  through a canyon of dark wet stone — near-black basalt walls, ferns and
  moss climbing every ledge, sprays of magenta and rose-pink blossoms
  clinging to the rock, white water crashing into blue-green pool below,
  and warm gold light breaking through the canopy far above. Quiet,
  damp, secret. A place you have to go looking for.

EXTENDING THE THEME TO NEW APPS

  When theming a new application, follow this decision tree:

  Background?       → hollow #0a1512 or deep #122620
  Surface/card?     → deep #122620 or shade #1e3a30
  Text?             → foam #eaf5ec (primary)  haze #7fa08f (secondary)
  Accent/highlight? → blossom #d1467e (strong)  petal #c97a97 (soft)
  Bright accent?    → orchid #ff74ab
  Border?           → blossom #d1467e (active)  shade #1e3a30 (inactive)
  Hover?            → dusk #2f5245
  Success?          → leaf #6ea86a
  Warning?          → gold #e8b84b
  Error?            → coral #d9506f
  Info?             → mist #a9d8d4
  Shadow/glow?      → rgba(d1467e, 0.25–0.40)

  Readability note (added after the Obsidian theme build):
  raw haze/dusk/shade/blossom/hollow read beautifully on a terminal
  bar or status line where the surrounding chrome is small and the
  eye isn't lingering, but they get muddy fast — or in hollow's case,
  tiring to read against — as body text, long-form borders, or any
  surface read continuously for more than a second (reading apps,
  editors, docs). For those contexts, swap in the derived tones
  instead:
    main reading surface   → stone #101f1a        (not raw hollow)
    secondary/muted text   → haze-text #9ab8ab    (not raw haze)
    inactive borders/hr    → border-soft #2a463c  (not raw shade)
    visible/hover borders  → border-strong #3d6656 (not raw dusk)
    accent needing to read
      at text size (links,
      headings, focus rings) → blossom-accent #e0568c or
                                 link-heading #f184ac (not raw blossom)
  Rule of thumb: if it's a UI accent glanced at (status icon, bar
  segment) use the raw palette; if it's read continuously (body
  text, link color, a border you have to find, the page you write
  on) use the lifted/desaturated tone.

  Rule: pure black (#000000) and pure white (#ffffff) are never used.
  The darkest point is hollow #0a1512, lightest is foam #eaf5ec.
  Every color has a green/damp-stone undertone — even the pinks and
  golds sit against a cool mossy base rather than a neutral one.
