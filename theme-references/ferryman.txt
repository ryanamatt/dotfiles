Ferryman Theme Reference

CONCEPT

  A death-and-water aesthetic built around a hooded reaper poling a
  boat through frozen marsh mist, one lantern the only warm thing for
  miles — extended into a full cohesive palette.

  The feeling: the crossing itself — a cold, ink-blue void of fog and
  reeds, a silhouette darker than the night around it, and a single
  amber lantern swinging at the prow, throwing everything it touches
  into gold. Runes still glow like old gold under frost. Ice-pale
  breath-fog drifts at the waterline. Somewhere behind the mist, the
  far shore.

EXTENDING THE THEME TO NEW APPS

  When theming a new application, follow this decision tree:

  Background?       → styx #050a10 or deep #0d1b2b
  Surface/card?     → deep #0d1b2b or shadow #16293d
  Text?             → pale #dceaf2 (primary)  mist #7e93a8 (secondary)
  Accent/highlight? → lantern #f2a83c (strong)  wisp #5a7a94 (soft)
  Bright accent?    → flame #ffcf6b
  Border?           → lantern #f2a83c (active)  shadow #16293d (inactive)
  Hover?            → dusk #1f3c54
  Success?          → wraith #5ecf9a
  Warning?          → rune #f5c842
  Error?            → blood #c9455a
  Info?             → rime #8fd9e0
  Shadow/glow?      → rgba(f2a83c, 0.25–0.40)

  Readability note (added after the Obsidian theme build):
  raw mist/dusk/shadow/lantern/styx read beautifully on a terminal
  bar or status line where the surrounding chrome is small and the
  eye isn't lingering, but they get muddy fast — or in styx's case,
  tiring to read against — as body text, long-form borders, or any
  surface read continuously for more than a second (reading apps,
  editors, docs). For those contexts, swap in the derived tones
  instead:
    main reading surface   → hollow #0f1a24        (not raw styx)
    secondary/muted text   → mist-text #93a8ba     (not raw mist)
    inactive borders/hr    → border-soft #2a3f52   (not raw shadow)
    visible/hover borders  → border-strong #3d5870 (not raw dusk)
    accent needing to read
      at text size (links,
      headings, focus rings) → lantern-accent #f5b354 or
                                 link-heading #ffc978 (not raw lantern)
  Rule of thumb: if it's a UI accent glanced at (status icon, bar
  segment) use the raw palette; if it's read continuously (body
  text, link color, a border you have to find, the page you write
  on) use the lifted/desaturated tone.

  Rule: pure black (#000000) and pure white (#ffffff) are never used.
  The darkest point is styx #050a10, lightest is pale #dceaf2.
  Every color has a cold blue undertone — even the amber lantern
  tones sit against, rather than blend into, the surrounding chill.
